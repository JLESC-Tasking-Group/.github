# JLESC
The Joint Laboratory for Extreme Scale Computing (JLESC) is an international, virtual organization dedicated to fostering collaborative research in high-performance computing (HPC). Its mission is to empower member institutions and researchers to effectively address the growing challenges of extreme-scale computing by advancing HPC, hybrid HPC/quantum and HPC-driven AI technologies. Within the JLESC, researchers advance HPC hardware, software, and methods to improve the use, the performance and scalability of traditional HPC and hybrid QC workloads, as well as of emerging AI workloads on HPC systems.

See [https://jlesc.github.io/](https://jlesc.github.io/) for more information.

# Project - Tasking Programming Models

Many task-based programming models are being developed to exploit complex distributed and heterogeneous systems. This project explores common low-level interfaces to mutualize efforts across institutions.

## Collaborating Institutions and Repositories
- Argonne National Laboratory (ANL, USA):  https://github.com/anlsys/
- Barcelona Supercomputing Center (BSC, Spain): https://github.com/bsc-pm
- National Institute for Research in Digital Science and Technology  (Inria, France): https://gitlab.inria.fr/starpu/starpu
- University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC, USA): https://github.com/UIUC-PPL

## Year 2026-2027 focus
We explore just-in-time graph optimization techniques (compilation, batching, etc.) and their integration in the programming models, notably OpenMP and OmpSs.
